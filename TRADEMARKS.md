# FarmSlicer trademark policy

FarmSlicer™ and the FarmSlicer logo (the "layer F" in `web/icons/`) are
trademarks of Cameron Tacklind, FarmSlicer's maintainer. They aren't
registered yet; the ™ says the name is claimed as a brand.

The code is free software under the AGPL (see `NOTICE`), and you can use,
change, and share it as that licence says. The licence covers the code, not
the name: the AGPL lets a project decline to grant trademark rights
(section 7(e)), and this one does. This policy says what you can do with the
name without asking. Its aim is that "FarmSlicer" always means this
project's software, so that people know what they're getting.

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what's true**: "works with FarmSlicer", "a plugin for
  FarmSlicer", "based on FarmSlicer", "we print with FarmSlicer", in
  articles, talks, reviews, docs, and the like.
- **Running FarmSlicer** for yourself, your farm, your club, your school, or
  your company's own people under its name, modified or not. Your users can
  see it called FarmSlicer.
- **Sharing unmodified releases** (the source, or the published images)
  under the name.
- **Packaging it** for an operating system or app store with only the
  changes needed to build and run it there, as long as you say who
  packaged it.

## Ask first

- **Distributing a modified version, or offering one as a service to the
  public**, under the name. Give your version its own name; you can say
  it's "based on FarmSlicer". (A modified version must already point its
  "Source code" link at your source: `FARMSLICER_SOURCE_URL`.)
- **Selling FarmSlicer hosting or support** under the name, or anything
  that suggests you're the project or endorsed by it.
- **Using the name or logo in your own** product, company, or domain
  names, or on merchandise.

To ask, open an issue at
[github.com/cinderblock/FarmSlicer](https://github.com/cinderblock/FarmSlicer).
Most reasonable requests get a yes.

## Using the name

Write it as one word with a capital F and S: FarmSlicer. Use it as a name
("FarmSlicer slices the plate"), not as a verb or a generic word for
slicing services. Don't change the logo beyond resizing it.

## Other people's marks

FarmSlicer is built on libslic3r, which comes from Slic3r by way of
PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, and OrcaSlicer, and it works with printers made
by others. OrcaSlicer, Slic3r, PrusaSlicer, Bambu Lab, Bambu Studio,
Prusa, Klipper, Moonraker, OctoPrint, Elegoo, and the other names used in
FarmSlicer and its documentation belong to their owners. FarmSlicer names
them only to say what it uses and what it works with, and isn't affiliated
with or endorsed by any of them.
